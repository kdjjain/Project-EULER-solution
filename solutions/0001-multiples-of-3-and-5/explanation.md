\# Problem 001 - Multiples of 3 and 5



\## Problem



Find the sum of all natural numbers below 1000 that are multiples of 3 or 5.



\## Approach



Iterate through every number from 1 to 999.



For each number:

\- Check if it is divisible by 3 or 5.

\- If yes, add it to the answer.



Finally, print the accumulated sum.



\## Complexity



Time Complexity: O(N)



Space Complexity: O(1)
